## Execution Assumption

Resting buys fill when the replayed best ask is less than or equal to the order price. Resting sells fill when the replayed best bid is greater than or equal to the order price. Each fill executes the configured order quantity at the resting order's posted limit price (standard maker matching: no price improvement). Orders fill only when the cumulative displayed quantity across all LOB levels that crossed the order price meets the order size (full-fill-or-nothing cumulative depth check).
